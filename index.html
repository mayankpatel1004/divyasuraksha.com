How It Works

5-Step Application Guide

Simple and safe instructions for optimal protective seal.

1

Prepare Area

Follow wound-care instructions provided by your healthcare professional.

2

Clean & Dry

Ensure surrounding skin is clean and completely dry before application.

3

Center Opening

Centre the raised dome opening directly over the wound area.

4

Press Border

Gently press adhesive border onto surrounding healthy skin to secure it.

5

Inspect Seal

Check seal regularly. Replace if loose, damaged, or contaminated.
