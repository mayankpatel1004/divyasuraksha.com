Engineering & Clinical Care

Why Choose CLARIDOME™ Protection Cap?

Key features designed to accelerate recovery, improve patient comfort, and simplify wound monitoring.

Zero Friction & Touch Protection

Raised dome structural vault creates physical space, guaranteeing clothes, bedding, or bandages do not rub against sensitive wound sites.

Clear Inspection Window

High-clarity central viewport allows doctors, nurses, and patients to easily inspect wound healing without removing or disturbing dressing.

Contaminant & Shock Barrier

Forms a robust protective shield guarding against external dust, water splashes, micro-contaminants, and inadvertent accidental contact.

Breathable & Hypoallergenic

Made with medical-grade skin-friendly adhesive that stays firmly secure while facilitating proper skin transpiration.

Waterproof Film Shield

Compatible with DIY waterproof film sheets for daily hygiene routines like showering, keeping wound dressings completely dry.

Multiple Ergonomic Sizes

Available in Standard (19x72mm) for minor incisions/sutures and Large (50x80mm) for surgical procedures and wider wounds.

Clinical Guidance

5-Step Application Guide

Simple instructions for applying CLARIDOME™ protection caps effectively.

1

Prepare Site

Clean and disinfect wound according to healthcare professional advice.

2

Dry Surrounding Skin

Ensure surrounding healthy skin is dry to facilitate optimal adhesion.

3

Center Dome Opening

Position the raised dome window directly over the central wound area.

4

Press Border Securely

Gently press down the adhesive border along all edges for a continuous seal.

5

Inspect & Monitor

Regularly inspect through the clear window; replace if seal is compromised.
